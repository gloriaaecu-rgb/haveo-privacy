Common Questions

How do I delete my data?

All your data lives on your device. Delete the app and everything is gone — we don't store anything on our servers.

Is my data shared with anyone?

Only when you use AI features — your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses. Nothing else leaves your phone. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The AI coach isn't responding.

Check your internet connection. The AI features require an active connection. If it still doesn't work, try closing and reopening the app.
