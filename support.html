Common Questions

How do I delete my data?

Everything you write lives on your phone. Delete the app and it is gone — we have no copy to delete, because we never had one. Individual events, notes, chats and contacts can be deleted inside the app.

What gets sent to the AI, and to whom?

When you ask Cici for something, what you typed goes to Anthropic, who write the reply — along with the event you are prepping for and any file you attach. When she reads a reply out loud, that one sentence goes to OpenAI, who make the voice. Neither company uses it to train their AI, and both delete it within about 30 days. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is sent for each feature.

How do I stop anything being sent?

Profile > AI > Let Cici answer, switched off. The chat, the file reading and the spoken voice stop straight away. Everything that runs on your phone alone — your events, notes, cheat sheet and reflections — keeps working exactly as before, and nothing you have saved is deleted.

The AI coach isn’t responding.

Check your internet connection — the AI features need one. If it still does not work, close the app and open it again. If Cici is switched off in Profile, she will say so rather than answer.

The practice partner is not speaking out loud.

If you are on an older version of the app, the spoken voice may be silent until you update — the conversation still works as text, and updating brings the voice back. Otherwise, check your ringer and volume, and that Cici is switched on in Profile.

I found a bug.

Thank you! Email us at haveoapp@gmail.com with a description of what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
