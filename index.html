Haveo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

TL;DR: Haveo is a personal networking-prep assistant. Everything you type stays on your device. We don't have a server that stores your data, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps.

What information Haveo collects

On your device only:

  • Your name, role, industry, and bio (the profile you set up during onboarding)
  • The events you add (name, date, time, description, your networking goals)
  • Notes, intros, talking points, and reflections you create
  • Your settings and preferences

This data is stored locally on your device using iOS's built-in storage. It never leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.

The microphone (only while you hold or tap to speak):

Haveo can listen when you choose to speak instead of type — dictating a message, or practising out loud in Role-Play. Nothing is recorded or stored: your speech is turned into text, the text is what the app uses, and the audio is discarded. iOS asks your permission the first time, and you can withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings.

Where your device supports transcribing speech on the phone itself, Haveo asks it to do so, and the audio never leaves your device. On devices or languages where that isn't available, iOS transcribes it using Apple's speech recognition service, which means the audio is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Haveo never receives or keeps the audio either way.

Sent to Anthropic (only when AI features are active):

When you chat with Haveo's AI coach (Coaching Studio, Role-Play, Talking Points), your messages are sent over HTTPS through a relay server we operate, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API and returns the response. The relay passes requests through without storing them. Anthropic processes those messages according to their own privacy policy.

We send Anthropic:

  • The text of your chat messages with the AI coach
  • Your name, role, industry, interests, and the event details you're prepping for (so the AI can give relevant advice)
  • The contents of any PDF or text file you choose to attach, so the AI can read it

We do not send Anthropic:

  • Your other events
  • Your reflections or quick notes
  • Your saved cheat sheet items
  • Anything from other parts of the app

If AI features are disabled, nothing is sent to Anthropic.

Sent to OpenAI (only when the AI reads something aloud):

Haveo's spoken voice is generated by OpenAI. When the AI coach reads a reply out loud, the text of that sentence is sent through the same relay to OpenAI to be turned into audio, and is processed under OpenAI's privacy policy. If you turn AI features off, your phone's own built-in voice is used instead and nothing is sent.

Asking first

Before anything is sent to Anthropic or OpenAI, Haveo asks you and waits for you to agree. Until you do, the AI features stay switched off — and everything that runs on your phone alone (your events, notes, cheat sheet and reflections) keeps working as normal.

You can change your mind whenever you like: Profile > AI > Let Cici answer. Switching it off stops the chat, file reading and the spoken voice immediately. Nothing you have already saved is deleted.

What Haveo does not do

  • We don't have user accounts or login
  • We don't collect analytics about how you use the app
  • We don't use cookies or tracking pixels
  • We don't share data with advertisers
  • We don't sell data to third parties
  • We don't access your camera, location, contacts, photos, or notifications
  • We don't have a backend server that stores your data (our relay only forwards AI requests without keeping them)

Your data, your control

  • Delete an event: removes it from your device immediately. We can't recover it.
  • Delete the app: removes everything Haveo has stored on your device.
  • Reset everything: iOS Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Haveo > Delete App.

There's no "delete my account" button because there are no accounts.

Children's privacy

Haveo is rated 13+ on the App Store (12+ in some regions and on earlier operating systems) and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
