Haveo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

TL;DR: Haveo is a personal networking-prep assistant. Everything you type stays on your device. We don't have a server that stores your data, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps.

What information Haveo collects

On your device only:

  • Your name, role, industry, and bio (the profile you set up during onboarding)
  • The events you add (name, date, time, description, your networking goals)
  • Notes, intros, talking points, and reflections you create
  • Your settings and preferences

This data is stored locally on your device using iOS's built-in storage. It never leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.

Sent to Anthropic (only when AI features are active):

When you chat with Haveo's AI coach (Coaching Studio, Role-Play, Talking Points), the messages you send are transmitted to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. Anthropic processes those messages according to their own privacy policy.

We send Anthropic:

  • The text of your chat messages with the AI coach
  • Your name, role, industry, and the event details you're prepping for (so the AI can give relevant advice)

We do not send Anthropic:

  • Your other events
  • Your reflections or quick notes
  • Your saved cheat sheet items
  • Anything from other parts of the app

If AI features are disabled, nothing is sent to Anthropic.

What Haveo does not do

  • We don't have user accounts or login
  • We don't collect analytics about how you use the app
  • We don't use cookies or tracking pixels
  • We don't share data with advertisers
  • We don't sell data to third parties
  • We don't access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or notifications
  • We don't have a backend server that holds your data

Your data, your control

  • Delete an event: removes it from your device immediately. We can't recover it.
  • Delete the app: removes everything Haveo has stored on your device.
  • Reset everything: iOS Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Haveo > Delete App.

There's no "delete my account" button because there are no accounts.

Children's privacy

Haveo is rated for ages 12+ and is not directed at children under 12. We don't knowingly collect any information from children under 12.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top and post the new version at this URL.
